Our Story

Pull up a chair.

The Tepperman Table & Co. started the way the best things do: around a long wooden table, with too much food and the people we love. Family recipes, a well-worn rolling pin, and a stubborn belief that real butter matters.

Today we bake small batches by hand, from scratch, every day. Nothing comes out of a box, and nothing leaves our kitchen unless we'd serve it at our own table.

“Good food tastes better in fine company. That's the whole recipe.”
